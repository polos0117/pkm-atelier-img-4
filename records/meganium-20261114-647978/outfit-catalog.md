# Our exposure situations — every situation we already use

For the free category + situation cut, invent a situation that is NOT on this list.

- wet — a sudden rain shower or a splash of water has just soaked her clothes — the damp fabric clings to her body line and darkens in patches, a few wet strands of hair stick to her neck and cheek; the fabric stays opaque
- wind — a strong gust of wind catches her clothes — the hem and every loose piece of fabric lift and flutter, pressing tight against her body line on the windward side, her hair blown to one side
- undone — she wears her top half undone — the zip or the top buttons open much lower than usual, so the neckline falls open over her collarbones and the inner curve of her chest, the loose edges slipping apart
- strap — one strap — or one side of the neckline — has slipped down off her shoulder, baring that whole shoulder and the top of her arm; she leaves it as it is
- knot — she has knotted the hem of her top up under her bust to keep it out of the way, baring her midriff and waist
